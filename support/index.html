Support

TradeBuilt HVACR Toolbox for iPhone and iPad

Get help

Email tradebuiltusa@gmail.com. We usually answer within two business days.

If something isn’t working right, include your device and iOS version — Settings → General → About. It saves a round trip.

Common questions

A number looks wrong

Tell us which calculator, what you entered, and what it handed back. Include the refrigerant if that screen uses one. That’s almost always enough to find it.

The caution card opens every time

Check the box on the card to keep it down for the rest of the session. To turn the notices off for good, tap the gear on the home screen and uncheck Show Important Notices.

My refrigerant isn’t listed

Send us the number. We add refrigerants as techs ask for them.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. One purchase covers iPhone and iPad on the same Apple Account.

Do I need a signal?

No. TradeBuilt works entirely offline — basements, crawlspaces, rooftops, mechanical rooms. Nothing in it needs a connection.

Refunds

Purchases run through Apple, so refunds do too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t issue them on our end.

Feature requests

Same address. Tell us what you’re doing on the truck that the app doesn’t cover yet — that’s where the next calculators come from.
