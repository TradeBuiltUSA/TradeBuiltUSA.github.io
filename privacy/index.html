Privacy Policy

Last updated August 7, 2026

The short version: TradeBuilt doesn’t collect anything.

TradeBuilt runs entirely on your phone. There’s no account to sign into, no server it talks to, and no code in it that sends anything anywhere. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking — not in this app, and not across any other.

What TradeBuilt stores

Two things: your notice settings, and whether you’ve bought the unlock. When you turn off Show Important Notices, or check the box to keep a caution card down for the rest of a session, that choice is saved on your device so the app remembers it. The unlock is saved the same way, so the app knows it’s paid for without having to ask Apple every time you open it. That’s the entire list.

It never leaves your phone. We can’t see it, and neither can anyone else.

Numbers you type into a calculator aren’t saved at all. They’re gone when you leave the screen.

What we don’t have

No name, no email, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no photos, no device identifier, no record of which calculators you use. We don’t have any of it because the app never asks for it and never sends anything.

Third parties

There are none. TradeBuilt contains no third-party code — no SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics libraries. Nobody else receives your data, because there’s no data to receive.

Apple

Apple handles the download and the payment, and collects its own information doing that. It’s covered by Apple’s privacy policy, not this one.

If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone’s analytics settings, Apple may also pass us anonymous crash diagnostics. That’s an Apple feature you control at Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and what reaches us has already had anything identifying stripped out of it by Apple.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Your settings go with it. There’s nothing on our end to erase.

Children

TradeBuilt is a work tool for HVACR technicians. It collects nothing from anyone, at any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes up on this page with a new date at the top.
