Privacy Policy

Last updated August 9, 2026

The short version: TradeBuilt doesn’t collect anything.

TradeBuilt runs entirely on your phone. There’s no account to sign into, no server of ours it talks to, and no code in it that sends your readings anywhere. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, not in this app and not across any other.

The one thing it reaches out to is Apple, to check whether your Apple Account has bought the unlock. That check carries nothing but the purchase.

What TradeBuilt stores

Four things, and all four stay on your phone.

Your notice settings. When you turn off Show Important Notices, or change the box on a caution card, that choice is saved so the app remembers it.

Whether you’ve bought the unlock. Saved so the app knows it’s paid for without having to ask Apple every time you open it.

The calculators and refrigerants you pin. The app writes down which ones you moved to the top so they’re still there next time.

A count of how many calculations you’ve run. That’s what tells the app when it has earned the right to ask you for a rating. It stops counting once it has asked.

That’s the entire list. None of it leaves your phone. We can’t see it, and neither can anyone else.

Numbers you type into a calculator are never written to your phone’s storage. They stay in the app’s memory, so a screen you walk off and come back to still has your readings on it. Closing the app clears them.

What we don’t have

No name, no email, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no photos, no device identifier. Nothing about how you use the app goes anywhere. The only thing it keeps about your calculators is the ones you pinned yourself, and that stays on the phone. We don’t have any of it because the app never asks for it and never sends it.

Third parties

There are none. TradeBuilt contains no third-party code. No SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics libraries. Nobody else receives your data, because there’s no data to receive.

Apple

Apple handles the download and the payment, and collects its own information doing that. It’s covered by Apple’s privacy policy, not this one.

If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone’s analytics settings, Apple may also pass us anonymous crash diagnostics. That’s an Apple feature you control at Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and what reaches us has already had anything identifying stripped out of it by Apple.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Your settings go with it. There’s nothing on our end to erase.

Children

TradeBuilt is a work tool for HVACR technicians. It collects nothing from anyone, at any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes up on this page with a new date at the top.
